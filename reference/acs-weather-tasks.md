# ACS Weather Tasks — Private, Instrument, Commercial

This is the official ACS text for the Weather Information task across all three ratings. CrosswindWX Learn pages should cite the relevant code(s) on every page, and ideally all three when content applies broadly (which is most of the time).

## At a glance

The Private and Commercial weather tasks are word-for-word identical except for the prefix. The Instrument task is also identical except for the prefix change and one additional skill element (S4) about alternate airport requirements. This means most Learn pages will cite all three codes simultaneously.

| Task | Private | Instrument | Commercial |
|------|---------|------------|------------|
| Section | Area I, Task C | Area I, Task B | Area I, Task C |
| Code prefix | `PA.I.C` | `IR.I.B` | `CA.I.C` |
| Special elements | — | adds S4 (alternate airport) | — |

## Private Pilot ACS — Area I, Task C: Weather Information

**References:** 14 CFR part 91; AC 91-92; AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25, FAA-H-8083-28

**Objective:** To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with weather information for a flight under VFR.

**Note:** If K2 is selected, the evaluator must assess the applicant's knowledge of at least three sub-elements.
**Note:** If K3 is selected, the evaluator must assess the applicant's knowledge of at least three sub-elements.

### Knowledge

- **PA.I.C.K1** — Sources of weather data (e.g., National Weather Service, Flight Service) for flight planning purposes.
- **PA.I.C.K2** — Acceptable weather products and resources for preflight planning, current and forecast weather:
  - **K2a** — Airport Observations (METAR and SPECI) and Pilot Observations (PIREP)
  - **K2b** — Surface Analysis Chart, Ceiling and Visibility Chart (CVA)
  - **K2c** — Terminal Aerodrome Forecasts (TAF)
  - **K2d** — Graphical Forecasts for Aviation (GFA)
  - **K2e** — Wind and Temperature Aloft Forecast (FB)
  - **K2f** — Convective Outlook (AC)
  - **K2g** — Inflight Aviation Weather Advisories (AIRMET, SIGMET, Convective SIGMET)
- **PA.I.C.K3** — Meteorology applicable to VFR/VMC flight, including hazardous conditions:
  - **K3a** — Atmospheric composition and stability
  - **K3b** — Wind (windshear, mountain wave, factors affecting wind)
  - **K3c** — Temperature and heat exchange
  - **K3d** — Moisture/precipitation
  - **K3e** — Weather system formation, including air masses and fronts
  - **K3f** — Clouds
  - **K3g** — Turbulence
  - **K3h** — Thunderstorms and microbursts
  - **K3i** — Icing and freezing level information
  - **K3j** — Fog/mist
  - **K3k** — Frost
  - **K3l** — Obstructions to visibility (smoke, haze, volcanic ash)
- **PA.I.C.K4** — Flight deck instrument displays of digital weather and aeronautical information.

### Risk Management

- **PA.I.C.R1** — Making the go/no-go and continue/divert decisions:
  - **R1a** — Circumstances that would make diversion prudent
  - **R1b** — Personal weather minimums
  - **R1c** — Hazardous weather conditions, including known or forecast icing or turbulence aloft
- **PA.I.C.R2** — Use and limitations of:
  - **R2a** — Installed onboard weather equipment
  - **R2b** — Aviation weather reports and forecasts
  - **R2c** — Inflight weather resources

### Skills

- **PA.I.C.S1** — Use available aviation weather resources to obtain an adequate weather briefing.
- **PA.I.C.S2** — Analyze the implications of at least three of the K3 conditions, using actual or evaluator-provided weather.
- **PA.I.C.S3** — Correlate weather information to make a go/no-go decision.

## Instrument Rating ACS — Area I, Task B: Weather Information

**References:** 14 CFR part 91; AC 91-92; AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25, FAA-H-8083-28

**Objective:** To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with obtaining, understanding, and applying weather information for a flight under IFR.

Same K1–K4 structure as Private (sub-elements identical, prefixed `IR.I.B` instead of `PA.I.C`).
Same R1–R2 structure.
Same S1–S3 — and **adds:**

- **IR.I.B.S4** — Determine whether an alternate airport is required, and, if required, whether the selected alternate airport meets regulatory requirements.

The K3 framing is for "IFR flight" rather than "VFR flight in VMC," but the sub-element list is identical.

## Commercial Pilot ACS — Area I, Task C: Weather Information

**References:** 14 CFR part 91; AC 91-92; AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25, FAA-H-8083-28

**Objective:** Identical to Private — focused on VFR/VMC.

Same K1–K4, R1–R2, S1–S3 structure as Private. Sub-elements word-for-word identical. Code prefix is `CA.I.C` instead of `PA.I.C`.

## Citation pattern on Learn pages

Use this format in the concept-page meta line, near the top of every Learn page:

```
ACS: PA.I.C.K2g · IR.I.B.K2g · CA.I.C.K2g    Risk: .R1c    Read time: ~8 min
```

The shorthand `.R1c` is acceptable since the prefix is the same as the K codes immediately before it.

## Primary sources

When citing facts, validity periods, criteria, or definitions on Learn pages, use these sources first. Don't cite from memory.

| Topic | Source | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Aviation weather products (METAR, TAF, AIRMET, SIGMET, etc.) — definitions, criteria, validity | **FAA-H-8083-28** *Aviation Weather Handbook* | The authoritative reference. AC 00-45H, the former authority, is cancelled and superseded by this Handbook — do not cite it. Used for the Convective SIGMET routine vs. special issuance criteria on the AIRMETs/SIGMETs page. |
| Pilot interpretation and use of weather products | **AIM Chapter 7** *Safety of Flight* | Operational, in the AIM. Useful for "how a pilot uses this product." |
| Aviation weather meteorology | **FAA-H-8083-28** *Aviation Weather Handbook* | Replaced the weather chapters of FAA-H-8083-25 and superseded AC 00-6B. Used for atmospheric concepts. |
| Live current weather products | **aviationweather.gov** | The Aviation Weather Center's site. Useful for examples but not for criteria or validity (those are in FAA-H-8083-28). |
| Regulations | **14 CFR Part 91** | FAR 91.103 covers preflight action including weather. |
